# Cedarbridge Bank - AI Use Policy
Version: DEMO-1.0 | Effective: 2026-09-01 | Owner: Security
SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY. Cedarbridge Bank is fictional. This is not a real bank policy or legal advice.

## AI-01 | Customer data in public AI tools | SecurityReview
Customer statements, account numbers, transaction histories, and other confidential customer data must not be uploaded to public AI chatbots. A request involving customer data and an external AI service must be routed to SecurityReview before any use. Creating a review case does not authorize the upload.

## AI-02 | Approved internal AI use | AIEnablement
Staff may summarize public marketing materials using the approved internal AI assistant. The materials must contain no customer data, employee personal information, or confidential business information. Questions about access to the approved internal AI assistant go to AIEnablement.

## AI-03 | AI policy exception request | SecurityReview
Requests for an exception to the AI Use Policy require a documented business purpose, data classification, proposed provider, and retention terms. SecurityReview owns the assessment. A reviewer may approve creation of an assessment case; only the designated policy owner can grant a policy exception outside this demo workflow.
