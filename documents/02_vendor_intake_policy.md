# Cedarbridge Bank - Vendor Intake Policy
Version: DEMO-1.0 | Effective: 2026-09-01 | Owner: Vendor Risk
SYNTHETIC DEMO POLICY. Cedarbridge Bank is fictional. This is not a regulatory standard.

## VR-01 | Missing vendor assurance report | VendorRisk
A new analytics or software vendor without a current SOC 2 Type II report must be referred to VendorRisk for an alternative assurance assessment before onboarding. The assessment request should include the vendor name, service description, intended data access, and available assurance evidence. A missing report is not automatic approval or automatic rejection.

## VR-02 | Vendor data residency | VendorRisk
A vendor proposing storage or processing of confidential bank data outside the approved hosting region requires a VendorRisk assessment. The requester must provide the proposed country or region and the categories of data involved. This demo policy does not enumerate approved regions; a country-specific approval question requires the approved-region register.

## VR-03 | Procurement pricing questions | Procurement
Questions about a vendor quotation, purchase order, or renewal price are routed to Procurement. This policy contains no price thresholds or guaranteed discount levels. Requests for a numeric approval threshold require the separate procurement authority schedule.
