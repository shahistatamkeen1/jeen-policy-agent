# Cedarbridge Bank - Policy Review Procedure
Version: DEMO-1.0 | Effective: 2026-09-01 | Owner: Operations
SYNTHETIC DEMO PROCEDURE. All examples and people in this project are fictional.

## OPS-01 | Human review and case creation | Operations
Before a policy review case is created, a human reviewer must inspect the request, the proposed queue, and the cited policy excerpts, then explicitly approve or reject case creation. A rejection creates no case. Approval authorizes only the creation of a review case, not a vendor onboarding, data transfer, purchase, or policy exception.

## OPS-02 | Missing knowledge | Operations
When the supplied documents do not support an answer, the assistant must state that it cannot determine the answer and request the relevant policy or more specific information. It must not invent a retention duration, review deadline, pricing threshold, approved country, or legal requirement. No operational review case is created from an unsupported recommendation in this demo.
